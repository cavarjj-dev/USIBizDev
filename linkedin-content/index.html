Ship checklist

  • No em dashes
  • Light contractions OK on LI only
  • ~130–180 words default
  • Two of four: process, stake, bridge, unfinished edge
  • No techno chassis / no propositional cascade
  • End on claim or residue, not bait Q
  • No photo of Jules
  • Kitchen table test

Stop: tip lists, humbled/excited opens, platform dunks, fee autobiography, batch-dump without scoring.
