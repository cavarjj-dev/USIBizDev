Operating system (reference)

Deliberate practice recipe

  1. One narrow target
  2. Edge of ability (~60–80% success)
  3. Full attention, short block (age 12: 15–25 min slices)
  4. Immediate feedback
  5. Adjust and repeat; stop when quality collapses
  6. One-line log

Youth rule

Deliberate play and breadth stay high in Phase A. Avoid early year-round single-sport max volume.

Adult rules

  • Environment > sermons
  • Defend sleep
  • Process/strategy praise, not trait praise
  • Agency ladder: show → together → he does → he plans
  • Art is not the spare tire
  • Measure access under pressure

Anti-patterns

  • Every hour optimized
  • Sleep as residual
  • Re-read as main study
  • Shame coaching
  • Parent owns 100% of motivation
